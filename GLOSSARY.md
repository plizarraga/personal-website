# Personal Website

Pedro Lizárraga's personal-brand website: who he is, what he has built, and how to reach him, readable at a glance by people, search crawlers, and LLMs.

## Audiences

**Primary audience**:
Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Pedro for remote Senior Full-Stack roles. The home page speaks to them first.
_Avoid_: visitors, users, readers

**Freelance client**:
A business that could hire Pedro to build an AI agent. Secondary audience, served by its own section, never by the home page's main message.
_Avoid_: customer, lead, prospect

## Identity

**Canonical name**:
"Pedro Lizárraga", the one display name used identically on the site, the CV, LinkedIn, and GitHub. "Pedro Lizárraga Brambila" and "Pedro Lizarraga" are alternate forms of the same person, not separate names.
_Avoid_: P. Lizárraga, plizarraga (that is a handle, not a name)

**Headline**:
The professional title line, "Senior Software Engineer | Full-Stack", worded identically wherever Pedro's identity appears.
_Avoid_: tagline, bio, title

**Differentiator**:
One of the two things that set Pedro apart from other full-stack engineers: healthcare platforms built on Epic FHIR and GraphQL, and AI agents. They qualify the Headline; they never replace it.
_Avoid_: specialty, niche, USP

**Domain**:
A kind of business or product where Pedro has built software, backed by a Case study: healthcare, B2B commerce, electronic invoicing, and AI agents. Healthcare and AI agents are the Differentiators.
_Avoid_: sector, industry, vertical, niche, finance (say electronic invoicing)

## Content

**CV Master**:
Pedro's master CV, the single source of every fact on the site. A fact, metric, or technology absent from the CV Master does not appear on the site.
_Avoid_: base CV, main CV, resume

**Role**:
A position Pedro held at one company, with a title and dates, as listed in the CV Master. A Role may contain Projects.
_Avoid_: job, position, experience (that is the page that lists the Roles)

**Project**:
One piece of work, done either within a Role or on Pedro's own as a selected project. A Project may be told in full by a Case study, but it is not one.
_Avoid_: portfolio item, product

**Case study**:
A page that explains one project in depth (context, Pedro's role, decisions, outcome), built only from CV Master facts.
_Avoid_: portfolio item, project page, post, article
